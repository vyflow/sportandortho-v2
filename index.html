01 / Why choose Sports & Ortho

Where you hurt is only
part of the picture.

Your knee doesn’t work alone. Your feet and hips help shape the way you step, squat and lift. That’s why we assess how you move beyond the painful spot, looking for connections that may be contributing to your symptoms.

Tell us what your day asks of you. A shift on your feet. Carrying equipment. Getting back to the gym. We use your movement assessment and your goals to shape care around what you need to do.

Meet your care team

What that means for you

Your body. Your demands.
A plan that connects them.

Here’s how our approach shapes your care, from your first assessment to the movements you practice.

  • Movement that carries over

    Your life moves
    in every direction.

    Reaching overhead. Turning with a load. Stepping off a curb. We connect your rehabilitation to the movements you use at work, at home and when you’re active.

  • Strength with control

    More than
    moving weight.

    Lifting matters. So does controlling the lift. Your plan brings strength, mobility and balance together around the demands you want to handle again.

  • A starting point that fits

    Start with what
    you can do.

    Your abilities matter as much as your difficulties. We build from where you are, explain what you’re working on and adapt your plan as your recovery progresses.

  1. 01 / Request your visit

    Start with what
    you need.

    Choose your clinic and leave a callback number. Tell the team whether you need help with a work injury, surgery recovery or getting active again.
