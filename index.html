Your first visit

Come as you are.
We’ll start together.

Talk through what’s been difficult, what you want to return to and how you’re moving today.

  • Bring your photo ID and insurance card, if you have one.
  • Wear comfortable clothes you can move in.
  • Bring any relevant medical reports or surgery instructions.

Your benefits are verified and explained before your first visit.
